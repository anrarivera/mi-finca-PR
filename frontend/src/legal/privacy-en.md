Mi Finca PR is an application for managing farms: fields, crops, animals, tasks, and production. This policy explains what data we keep, why, and what control you have over it.

## What we collect

**Account data.** Your name, email address, and password (stored only as an encrypted hash — we can never see your password). Also your preferred language and notification preferences.

**Your farm's data.** What you record to run your farm: farm names and locations, **GPS coordinates of farm, field, and corral boundaries**, crops and plantings, animals and their counts, completed tasks, pest findings, harvests and production — including **sale prices if you choose to record them**.

**Team data.** If you invite people to your farm (or join one), we store that relationship: who is a member of which farm, with what role, and who logged each task or finding.

**What we do NOT collect.** We use no advertising trackers, third-party analytics, or tracking cookies. The only cookie we set is the session cookie (essential to keep you signed in). We do not sell data — anyone's, ever.

## How we use your data

- To operate the application: showing your farm on the map, computing task calendars, generating reports.
- To send service emails: account verification, password resets, team notices, and the daily task digest (which you can turn off in Settings → Notifications).
- Nothing else. No advertising, no commercial profiling.

## Who it is shared with

**Your team, by design.** Members of a farm see that farm's data according to their role, including the name of whoever logged each entry. You control who is a member.

**Providers we need to operate** (data processors):
- Server and database hosting: [HOSTING PROVIDER].
- Email delivery: Resend, Inc. (receives your email address, name, and the content of service messages).
- Maps: satellite imagery comes from Esri/ArcGIS; when the map loads, your browser requests tiles directly from their servers (exposing your IP address to them, as with any website).

**No one else**, except under legal obligation (a valid court order).

## Where and how it is protected

Passwords are stored with bcrypt hashing. Data access is limited by roles (owner/admin/operator). Connections use TLS encryption. Data is hosted on [HOSTING PROVIDER / REGION] servers.

## Your rights and controls

- **Access and correction:** all your data is visible and editable inside the application.
- **Export:** you can download your tasks, health log, and production as CSV from the Field Log.
- **Deletion:** you can delete your account from Settings → Account. This permanently deletes your account, your farms, and all their data. If you were a member of someone else's farm, your membership is removed and your name is detached from the records you created (the farm's records remain, without your identity).
- **Email digests:** turn them off in Settings → Notifications.

## Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, deletion is immediate and permanent in the active database; backups rotate within at most [BACKUP PERIOD, e.g. 30 days].

## Minors

The service is intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
